Theme: “Survival Analysis and Machine Learning in Health Research”
Dates: July 14th-17th, 2025
Host: AMPATH / Moi University School of Medicine
Location: EKA & Noble Hotel, Eldoret, Kenya

NAMBARI, the Moi-Brown Partnership for HIV Biostatistics Training, and the Data Science for Social Determinants program held a training workshop that was primarily hands-on and centered around group-based activities rather than traditional lectures. Activities focused on teaching students different techniques in machine learning and survival analysis that they can apply to their work. A brief meeting was also held during the workshop to discuss the Social Determinant Working Group for those interested in getting involved.

Funding: Financial support is provided by the Fogarty International Center of the US National Institutes of Health through grants D43TW010050 and 5U2RTW012121.

Instructors

  • Rumi Chunara, PhD is Associate Professor of Biostatistics and Computer Science, and Director of the Center for Health Data Science at New York University. rumi.chunara@nyu.edu
  • Joseph Hogan, ScD is Professor of Biostatistics and Deputy Director of the Data Science Initiative at Brown University. jhogan@stat.brown.edu
  • Ann Mwangi, PhD is an Associate Professor of Biostatistics, School of Science and Aerospace Studies, Moi University and Adjunct Assistant Professor at Brown University.
  • Ziv Shkedy, PhD is a Professor of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at Hasselt University in Belgium. ziv.shkedy@uhasselt.be
