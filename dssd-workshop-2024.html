Theme: “Machine Learning for Real-World Data”
Dates: October 7th-10th, 2024
Host: AMPATH / Moi University School of Medicine
Location: EKA Hotel, Eldoret, Kenya

NAMBARI, the Moi-Brown Partnership for HIV Biostatistics Training, and the Data Science for Social Determinants program held a five-day course on machine learning and predictive inference, with emphasis on topics in HIV. The course included a meeting on the last day (October 10th) for the Social Determinant Working Group (Community of Practice).

Funding: Financial support is provided by the Fogarty International Center of the US National Institutes of Health through grants D43TW010050 and 5U2RTW012121.

Instructors

  • Rumi Chunara, PhD is Associate Professor of Biostatistics and Computer Science, and Director of the Center for Health Data Science at New York University.
  • Joseph Hogan, ScD is Professor of Biostatistics and Deputy Director of the Data Science Initiative at Brown University.
  • Ann Mwangi, PhD is an Associate Professor of Biostatistics, School of Science and Aerospace Studies, Moi University and Adjunct Assistant Professor at Brown University.
  • Allison DeLong, MSc is a Biostatistician at the Brown University School of Public Health.
  • Ziv Shkedy, PhD is a Professor of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at Hasselt University in Belgium.
